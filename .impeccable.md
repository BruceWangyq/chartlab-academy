## Design Context

Source of truth: `chartlab_academy_full_prd_v1_0.docx` supplied by the product owner. Until the owner confirms the open brand and launch-market decisions, this file records the PRD's working direction rather than inventing alternatives.

### Users

Chinese-speaking beginners and intermediate learners who want to understand technical analysis through structured education rather than signals. They use the product to learn a concept, apply it to a hidden-future historical chart, receive explainable feedback, and review their own reasoning. The experience must never require brokerage, holdings, income, account size, or return-goal information.

### Brand Personality

Clear, disciplined, and honest. The interface should create calm confidence and intellectual curiosity, not urgency, fear of missing out, or fantasies of guaranteed returns. Copy separates observable facts from interpretation, states uncertainty and invalidation conditions, and repeatedly makes the education-only boundary clear.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined editorial learning product with an industrial chart-workbench edge. Use night navy for focused chart and onboarding moments, lightly indigo-tinted paper surfaces for reading, restrained indigo as the primary action color, and teal/coral/amber only for semantic feedback. Prefer asymmetric editorial composition, precise rules, real chart structures, compact labels, and strong typographic hierarchy. Working brand: 图表研习社 / ChartLab Academy.

Avoid the look of a brokerage terminal, crypto casino, signal seller, generic SaaS dashboard, cyan-purple neon AI product, glassmorphism, decorative performance metrics, fake urgency, profit screenshots, and red/green-only meaning. No default dark mode across long-form reading; dark surfaces are reserved for focus and contrast.

### Design Principles

1. Learning before spectacle: every visual element should support understanding, practice, or review.
2. Evidence before conclusion: show location, structure, context, and invalidation; never imply certainty or future performance.
3. Progressive depth: keep first actions simple, then reveal charts, annotations, explanations, and history without hiding essential controls.
4. Accessible by construction: target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-complete flows, visible focus, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion support, textual and tabular chart alternatives, captions/transcripts, and color-independent meaning.
5. Desktop practice, mobile continuity: the chart lab is desktop-first, while mobile must preserve viewing, numeric/non-drag input, submission, and review without horizontal overflow.
6. Performance is part of trust: public initial compressed JavaScript stays below 180 KB, chart code is deferred, and state changes must be recoverable on weak connections.

### Open Product Decisions

- Final approval of the working Chinese/English brand, domain, and trademark.
- Launch market and legal jurisdiction.
- Licensed historical-data provider and redistribution scope.
- Final instructor identity, video production style, and named reviewer team.

These are go-live gates; they must not be silently resolved through visual or copy changes.
